Part One
The Turn
From a supply-side promise to people-centered policymaking, the case for putting people inside abundance.
SUPPLY — built, but for whom?
01 · THE PREMISE

What happens when…you build for abundance?

Abundance starts as a supply-side promise: clear the blockers, move faster, build the housing and energy and transit the future needs. A grid of capacity, expanding. Powerful — and, on its own, incomplete.

02 · THE GAP

What happens when…you notice who's missing?

Three years in, a pattern surfaced. Supply-side reform kept answering how much and how fast — and skipping for whom, and decided by whom. The grid was cold. The people weren't in it yet.

"There was something missing from the conversation."
03 · IN GOOD FAITH

What happens when…two philosophies meet honestly?

The Lab brought one expertise — community engagement and state capacity. A consortium of field experts brought another — the policy itself. Two streams, kept honest and separate, then merged on purpose.

"When our philosophy comes to this other philosophy in good faith — here's what we learned."
04 · THE TURN

What happens when…you put people at the center?

The cold grid warms and gathers. Abundance doesn't get abandoned — it gets completed. The destination has a name now: people-centered policymaking. The same goal, with the people inside it.

"We started with abundance and landed on people-centered policymaking. Here's how."
